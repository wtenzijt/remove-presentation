# Making Trust Visible

A 6-minute walkthrough of the strategy and operations behind Climate Cooperative, for the Head of Strategy & Operations interview at remove.

**Open the deck:** https://wtenzijt.github.io/remove-presentation/

## Presenting
1. Open the deck and press `P`: a presenter window opens with the speaker notes, the next slide and a timer.
2. Share only the **deck window/tab**, not your whole screen. Keep the presenter window on your own screen.
3. Press `F` in the deck for fullscreen. Arrow keys work in either window; both stay in sync.
